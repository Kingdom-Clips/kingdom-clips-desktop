# Kingdom Clips for Mac and Windows

Kingdom Clips turns sermons and podcasts into short clips and schedules them.
This app is everything in one download: no Chrome extension and no separate
Companion to install.

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/Kingdom-Clips/kingdom-clips-desktop/releases/latest)**

| Your computer | Download |
| --- | --- |
| Mac with Apple silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4 and newer) | `Kingdom-Clips-…-mac-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `Kingdom-Clips-…-mac-x64.dmg` |
| Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) | `Kingdom-Clips-Setup-…-win-x64.exe` |

Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu → About This Mac. "Chip: Apple…" means
Apple silicon; "Processor: Intel…" means Intel.

The Mac app needs macOS 13 Ventura or later.

## First launch

The app is not yet signed by Apple or Microsoft, so your computer asks you to
approve it once.

### Mac

1. Open the `.dmg` and drag **Kingdom Clips** into **Applications**. Open it
   from Applications.
2. macOS says **"Kingdom Clips" Not Opened** and that Apple could not verify
   it. This does not mean anything harmful was found. Choose **Done** (not
   Move to Trash).
3. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and choose
   **Open Anyway** next to Kingdom Clips. Confirm with your password or
   Touch ID.
4. Kingdom Clips opens. You only do this once.

### Windows

1. Double-click **Kingdom-Clips-Setup-…-win-x64.exe**.
2. If Windows says **Windows protected your PC**, choose **More info**, then
   **Run anyway**.
3. Kingdom Clips installs by itself (no administrator needed) and opens.

Sign in with your Kingdom Clips account.

## Updates

Windows updates itself. On a Mac, Kingdom Clips downloads the new version and
asks you to restart when it is ready. The app's screens come from
kingdomclips.org, so most improvements arrive without an update at all.

## Licenses and source code

Kingdom Clips bundles yt-dlp, Deno and FFmpeg. Their licenses are inside the
app and attached to every release, together with the complete source code of
the FFmpeg build it ships.
